## 核心领域

```text
Customer Domain (Sales所有)
    ├── Customer
    ├── Contact
    ├── Inquiry
    └── SalesOrder

Supplier Domain (Buyer所有)
    ├── Supplier
    ├── SupplyResource
    ├── Offer
    └── PurchaseOrder

Finance Domain (Finance所有)
    ├── AR
    ├── AP
    ├── Credit
    └── RiskCase

Shared Domain (ANOS所有)
    ├── Product
    ├── Opportunity
    ├── Knowledge
    └── Agent
```

## 域边界

- Customer Domain 与 Supplier Domain 通过 Opportunity 间接关联
- 禁止直连: Customer↔Supplier, Sales↔PurchaseCost
